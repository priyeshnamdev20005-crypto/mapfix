Justdial vs Google Business Profile: which one brings customers?

LOCAL SEO · 18 Sept 2026 · 6 MIN READ

Almost every small business owner in India has had the Justdial call: a package, a number of leads, a yearly figure. The question is whether it is worth it when Google gives you a listing for free.

Here is the honest comparison, from someone who sells Google listing work and therefore has an obvious bias — so we have tried to be fair about where Justdial genuinely wins.

The basic difference

Google shows your business to someone who searched for what you do. Justdial takes someone who searched for what you do and passes them to several businesses at once. That single difference explains most of what follows.

Where Google wins

  • It is free, permanently. Listing, photos, posts, reviews, messages — no package, no renewal.
  • It is where people look. For most categories, the map pack at the top of a Google search gets far more eyes than a directory page does.
  • The lead is yours alone. When someone taps Call on your profile, the phone rings in your shop and nowhere else.
  • Reviews stay with you. Your Google reviews show up on Maps, in search, and in the panel next to your name forever.
  • It feeds everything else. Directions, opening hours, photos, the website link, WhatsApp — all in one box.

Where Justdial genuinely wins

  • Habit, in some categories. There is still a set of customers — often older, often for home services, movers and repairs — who call Justdial rather than search.
  • Someone calls you. The enquiry lands as a phone call or SMS. If you have nobody to watch a Google profile, that is a real convenience.
  • It works without any effort from you. A Google profile rewards being maintained. Justdial does not ask you to do anything after you pay.

What a paid package actually costs

Packages are negotiated, not published, and the figure quoted to a salon in Bhayandar is not the one quoted to a clinic in Bandra. They range from a few thousand rupees a year for a basic verified listing to lakhs for top placement in a busy category in a metro, and the price is usually negotiable, especially near the end of a sales month.

Before you sign: ask for the number of enquiries per month in writing, ask how many other businesses each enquiry is sent to, ask what happens to your placement if you do not renew, and ask for a one-month or three-month trial before a yearly commitment. If none of that is available in writing, that is your answer.

What we actually recommend

  1. Claim your free Justdial listing and fix it. Name, address and phone must match your Google profile exactly — the same spelling, the same shop number, the same phone. Mismatched details across directories is one of the things that quietly holds down local ranking.
  2. Do the same on Sulekha, IndiaMART, Bing Places and Apple Maps. All free, all citations, and almost nobody bothers — which is exactly why it is worth doing.
  3. Put the work into Google first. Category, services, photos, description, reviews, posts. That is the free asset with the highest ceiling.
  4. Only then consider paying Justdial, and only in a category where you know customers still call directories — and only on a short trial you can walk away from.

The trap to avoid

The worst outcome we see is a business paying Justdial every year while its Google listing sits with the wrong category, no services and four unanswered reviews. That is paying rent on a rented shop while your own shop stands empty with the shutter half down.

Fix the free one first. Our free 15-point check tells you exactly what is missing on it, and whether your details match across directories — no cost, no call, nothing to sign. If it turns out a one-time repair is all you need, we will say that.

Straight answers

Questions people ask about this

Is Justdial worth paying for in India?

For a few categories where buyers still call Justdial first — packers and movers, some home services, AMC contracts — a paid package can pay for itself. For most retail, clinics, salons, studios and professional services, the same money spent on the Google profile and a simple website brings more. Ask for the leads-per-month figure in writing before you sign anything.

Does a Justdial listing help my Google ranking?

A little, and not in the way people think. It is a citation: it tells Google that your name, address and phone number appear consistently on another site. That helps only if the details match your Google listing exactly. A Justdial entry with an old phone number actively hurts.

Is a free Justdial listing worth having?

Yes. Claim it, correct the details so they match your Google profile character for character, and leave it. The free listing costs nothing and fixing a wrong one is worth the twenty minutes.

Why do I get calls from other vendors after a Justdial enquiry?

Because that is the model — an enquiry is typically sent to several businesses in the category at once. The customer gets calls from all of you within minutes. It is not a fault in your listing.
