What people ask before signing up

What does Google Business Profile management include each month?

Every new review answered within 24 hours, four Google posts, four new photos, the Products tab and Q&A kept current, your Justdial, Sulekha, IndiaMART, Bing Places and Apple Maps listings kept identical to your Google one, and a one-page monthly report showing calls, direction requests and searches. Is there a lock-in contract?

No. Every plan is month to month. Tell us before the next month starts and manager access is handed straight back, with everything that was built staying yours.

What is the difference between the Rs 3,500, Rs 9,000 and Rs 18,000 plans?

Found (Rs 3,500) is Google and directories only. Seen (Rs 9,000) adds a free one-page website, Instagram and Facebook posting, Reels and WhatsApp Business. Sold (Rs 18,000) adds a full website up to eight pages, logo and brand kit, SEO with a monthly blog article, and Google plus Meta ads with lead tracking. Full breakdown is on the pricing page.

Yes, and the money stays in your own Google Ads or Meta account, paid by your own card. We manage the account; we never hold your budget. You see every rupee in your own dashboard.
