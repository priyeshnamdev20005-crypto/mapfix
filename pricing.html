How a month actually runs

WEEK 1 · SETUP

You add us as manager on Google (and Meta on Seen/Sold). Full profile audit and repair. Website build starts. Content calendar for the month sent for your approval.

WEEK 2 · LIVE

Website goes live (Seen/Sold). First posts and Reels go up. Old reviews cleared. Directories set up. Ads campaign built and switched on (Sold).

WEEKS 3–4 · RUNNING

Posts on schedule, every new review answered within a day, comments and DMs handled, Google posts and photos added, ads watched daily and adjusted.

MONTH END · REPORT

One-page report in plain language: calls, direction requests, searches, followers, leads, what worked. Strategy call. Next month's calendar.
