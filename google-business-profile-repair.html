What "repair" actually means

Not "optimisation". A list of specific fields, each one either right or wrong, each one fixed.

Categories

The single biggest lever on a listing. "Skin care clinic" and "Dermatologist" show up for completely different searches. We set the primary category to what your best customers actually search, and add every secondary category you are missing.

Services, written to be searchable

Entries like "Home Services" tell Google nothing. Every service gets rewritten in the words customers type, and each one gets its own description.

Business description

750 characters most listings leave empty. Written with your real services and your area named, in plain language, without keyword stuffing that gets listings suspended.

Every unanswered review

Including the angry ones. An unanswered complaint sits at the top of your listing for years and is often the first thing a new customer reads. We write an individual reply to each one, referencing what the person actually said.

Call and WhatsApp buttons

We have opened listings with no phone number at all — every customer who found them on Maps had no way to call. Buttons switched on and tested.

Website link, hours, photos, Products tab

Dead links removed (worse than none), holiday hours set, photos uploaded, and the Products tab and attributes filled — free space almost nobody uses.

What it will not do: it will not guarantee you a ranking position, and anyone promising that is guessing. What a repair reliably changes is that Google finally knows what you sell, and customers who find you can reach you.
