Straight answers

Why are your prices lower than local agencies?

Because we are in Mumbai, India, where the cost of running a studio is a fraction of London or Austin. The work — and the tools, Google, Meta, WordPress — are exactly the same. You are not paying for our office rent.

How do I pay?

Wise, PayPal, or bank transfer, in USD. An invoice for every payment. One-time work is 50% to start, 50% on approval. Monthly plans are paid at the start of the month and can be stopped any month.

Do you need my passwords?

No. Google Business Profile, Meta and most hosting have a manager or collaborator role — you add us from your own account and can remove us any time.

Is your English good enough for my customers?

Everything we write for you — posts, replies, website copy — is written in clear, natural English and sent to you for approval before it goes anywhere. Calls happen on Google Meet when you want them; most clients prefer WhatsApp and email.
