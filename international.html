Prices, and what each one includes

1. Google Business Profile

This is the box that appears beside your name on Google and the pin that appears on Maps. For a local business it is usually the single biggest source of calls, and it is free — which is exactly why nobody maintains it. A repair means every field, not a tidy-up.

  • Primary and secondary categories corrected — the field that decides which searches you appear for
  • Business name brought in line with Google policy so it stops being a suspension risk
  • Description rewritten with the words your customers actually type
  • Every service listed separately, each with its own description
  • Products, attributes, holiday hours, service areas
  • Call, WhatsApp, booking and website buttons wired correctly
  • Every unanswered review given a proper owner reply
  • Duplicate listings found and merged, dead links removed
  • A before-and-after sheet showing every field that changed
What we need from you

Manager access to the profile (you add us, no password), your correct hours, and any photos you want used. That is it. Turnaround is five working days from approval.

Straight answers

Straight answers before you send anything

Why are your prices lower than agencies in my country?

Because MapFix is in Mumbai, India, where the cost of running a studio is a fraction of London, Dubai or Austin. The work and the tools - Google, Meta, WordPress - are exactly the same. You are paying for the work, not for an office in your city.

How do I pay from outside India?

Wise, PayPal or bank transfer, in US dollars, with a proper invoice every time. One-time work is 50% to start and 50% on approval. Monthly plans are paid at the start of the month and can be stopped any month. There are no setup fees and no minimum contract.

Do you need my passwords?

No, and we will not accept them if you offer. Google Business Profile, Meta, WordPress and most hosting all have a manager or collaborator role - you add hello@mapfix.in from inside your own account, and you can remove it in two clicks whenever you want.

Do you do white-label work for agencies?

Yes, and it is a large part of what we do. Agencies in the US, UK, Australia and the Gulf send us overflow - a landing page, a five-page site, a Google Business Profile cleanup - and deliver it under their own brand. Landing page $79, five-page site $199, profile repair $45, monthly posting $99. We never contact your client and our name appears nowhere.

Is your English good enough for my customers?

Everything we write for you - posts, review replies, website copy - comes to you in writing for approval before it is published anywhere. Nothing goes live that you have not read. Calls happen on Google Meet when you want them; most clients prefer WhatsApp and email, which also gives both of us a record.

What if the time difference is a problem?

It usually works in your favour. India is ahead of the Gulf, Europe and the Americas, so work you send at the end of your day is often finished when you wake up. Our working hours are 9am to 10pm India time, Monday to Saturday, which overlaps the whole Gulf working day, most of the UK and European day, and the US morning.

Who owns the website and the accounts?

You do, always. The domain is registered in your name on your own card, the hosting account is yours, and you keep every file. If you stop working with us you lose nothing and nothing has to be rebuilt.

Can you guarantee I will rank first on Google?

No, and nobody honestly can. What we guarantee is that the work listed on this page is done properly and that you get a before-and-after sheet showing every field that changed. Anyone promising a number one position is selling you something they cannot deliver.
