Questions

Things people ask before they message

Is the listing check really free?

Yes. Send a business name; you get a one-page PDF with all 15 checks and what each problem is likely costing. No call is required and nothing is signed. Many people fix it themselves from the report.

Do you need my Google password?

No, and please never give it to anyone. Google Business Profile has a Manager role — you add us from your own account, and you can remove us any time. Same for Instagram through Meta Business Suite.

Can you guarantee I will rank first?

No, and nobody honestly can. What we can guarantee is that every one of the 15 items will be fixed and you will get a before/after sheet showing each field that changed.

I am not in Mumbai. Does that matter?

Not at all. Everything happens over WhatsApp and a call. The only thing we need to see is your listing, and that is public.

What if I want to stop?

Monthly plans are month-to-month. Tell us before the next month starts; manager access is handed back and you keep everything that was built.

Where does my ad money go?

Into your own Google Ads or Meta account, paid by you. We manage the account; we never hold the budget. You can see every rupee in your own dashboard.
