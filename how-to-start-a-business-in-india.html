How to start a new business in India and get it found online

STARTING UP · 18 Sept 2026 · 9 MIN READ
Please read this first. What follows is general information to help you know what to ask about, not legal, tax or financial advice. Rules, thresholds and fees change, and what applies to you depends on your state, your turnover and your trade. Confirm everything here with a chartered accountant or your bank before you act on it. We are a digital services business, not advisers.

Starting a business in India splits into two halves that people usually do in the wrong order. There is the paperwork half — registrations, a bank account, GST — and there is the found-by-customers half. Most new owners spend two months on the first and start the second only when the money runs low.

Do them in parallel. The paperwork lets you take money legally. The online setup is what decides whether anybody calls.

Part one — the paperwork

1. Choose the structure

  • Sole proprietorship — you and the business are the same person. Simplest and cheapest to start; you are personally liable for the debts. Most small shops, salons, tutors and freelancers start here.
  • Partnership — two or more people with a partnership deed. Still personally liable.
  • LLP — a partnership with limited liability and its own annual filings.
  • Private limited company — a separate legal entity, the structure investors expect, with directors, audits and annual compliance costs.

A CA will match this to your actual numbers in one conversation. It is worth the fee — changing structure later is more expensive than choosing right now.

2. The registrations to ask about

  • PAN — yours for a proprietorship; the company’s for an LLP or Pvt Ltd.
  • Udyam (MSME) — free on the government portal, and useful as business proof at the bank.
  • Shop and Establishment registration — state level, needed by most premises-based businesses. Cost and process differ by state.
  • GST — where your turnover or your trade requires it. See the FAQ below, and ask a CA.
  • Trade licence from your municipal corporation, for many retail and food businesses.
  • FSSAI — compulsory for anything to do with food, including a home kitchen or a cloud kitchen.
  • Professional tax — applies in some states, including Maharashtra.
  • Sector licences — clinics, pharmacies, salons, schools and coaching classes all have their own. Ask someone in your trade what they needed.

3. Open a current account

Keep business money out of your personal savings account from day one — it makes tax time straightforward and it is what the bank will expect. Banks usually want two independent proofs that the business exists; ask your branch for their exact list, because it varies. Get a business UPI QR at the same time.

Part two — the half that brings customers

This is the part we do for a living, so here is the order we would actually follow, cheapest and highest-return first.

1. Google Business Profile — free, do it first

If customers come to you, or you travel to an area, this is the single highest-return afternoon of work available to a new business in India. It puts you on Maps, in the box beside your name in search, and in the "near me" results. It costs nothing. The full process is in our step-by-step guide.

2. A domain and a business email — about Rs 700 a year

Book the name before someone else does. A .in domain is roughly Rs 500–900 a year, and a business email on it — you at yourbusiness.in — is free on Zoho Mail’s forever-free plan. Writing from a business address instead of a Gmail changes how customers read your quotes.

3. WhatsApp Business — free

Switch your business number to the WhatsApp Business app: catalogue, business hours, greeting message, quick replies, labels. For most Indian small businesses this is where the actual selling happens.

4. Instagram, and Facebook if your customers are there

One clear profile photo, a bio that says what you do and where, your phone number, your link. Then post your work — real photos, not stock. Nine good posts beat ninety recycled ones.

5. Free directory listings

Justdial, Sulekha, IndiaMART, Bing Places, Apple Maps. Claim them free and make the name, address and phone identical to your Google listing, character for character. Whether the paid Justdial package is worth it is a separate question — we went through it here.

6. A website, when you have something to show

Not on day one. Once you have photos of real work and settled prices, five pages is enough — and you can do it yourself for the price of the domain. Our website guide has the three routes and what each should cost.

7. Reviews, from your very first customer

Ask every one of them. A business with twenty reviews in its first year looks established; the same business with two looks new and risky. The method is here, and it costs nothing.

A sane first thirty days

  1. Week 1 — talk to a CA, pick the structure, start the registrations. Book the domain the same day.
  2. Week 2 — create the Google Business Profile and begin verification. Set up WhatsApp Business. Set up the business email.
  3. Week 3 — open the current account and the UPI QR. Fill the Google profile properly: category, services, description, photos.
  4. Week 4 — claim the free directory listings. Set up Instagram. Ask your first customers for reviews.

Straight answers

Questions people ask about this

Do I need to register a company to start a small business in India?

Not necessarily. A sole proprietorship is not a separate company and needs no incorporation — you trade in your own name with the relevant local registrations. A private limited company or LLP is a separate legal entity with its own filings and annual costs. Which one suits you depends on your turnover, your risk and whether you want outside investment, so confirm it with a chartered accountant before you register anything.

Is GST registration compulsory for a new business?

It depends on turnover and what you do. The widely applied thresholds are Rs 40 lakh a year for goods and Rs 20 lakh for services in most states, with lower limits (Rs 20 lakh and Rs 10 lakh) in special category states. Some situations require registration from day one whatever your turnover — selling through an e-commerce platform, for example. Thresholds and exceptions change, so check the current position with a CA rather than relying on a blog.

What is Udyam registration and does it cost anything?

Udyam is the government’s MSME registration. It is free on the official udyamregistration.gov.in site, takes a few minutes with Aadhaar and PAN, and gives you a certificate that banks accept as business proof. Sites that charge you a fee for it are middlemen, not the government.

Can I open a current account for a sole proprietorship?

Yes. Banks generally ask for two independent proofs that the business exists — commonly a GST certificate, Udyam certificate, Shop and Establishment registration, a trade licence or a professional body registration. Requirements differ by bank, so ask your branch for their exact list before you go.

What should I do online first?

The Google Business Profile, if customers can come to you or you serve a local area. It is free, it takes an afternoon, and for most local businesses it brings more enquiries in the first month than anything else you could set up. Our step-by-step guide covers it.
