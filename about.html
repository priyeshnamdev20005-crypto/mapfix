Small on purpose

MapFix exists because our founder fixed his own Google listing, saw how much had been quietly wrong for years, and realised most small businesses are in the same spot.

The story

It started with one broken listing

Our founder runs a small local business in Mumbai. For two years its Google profile had the wrong service names, eighteen unanswered reviews, a dead website button and an ad account quietly blocking its own customers with 119 negative keywords nobody had ever read.

One day was enough to fix it. The ad budget dropped 59% for the same local reach. Every review got a reply. The posts that Google had been rejecting for a year went live once the phone number came out of the text.

That is the whole idea behind MapFix: most small businesses do not need a marketing agency. They need someone to open the listing, find the fifteen things that are wrong, fix them properly, and tell the truth about what that changes.

Deliberately small

MapFix takes a limited number of clients at a time, and the person who audits your listing is the person who repairs it. Your account does not get passed down a chain, and you are not handed a ticket number when you have a question.

What we will not promise

A ranking position, a number of calls, or results in 30 days. Anyone promising that is guessing. What you get before you pay a rupee is a written list of exactly what is wrong — and what fixing it normally changes.
