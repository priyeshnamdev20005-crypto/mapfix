Before MapFix took a single client, we repaired our founder's own Google profile and ad account in one day — and wrote down every fix, including the one still open. This is that list.

Ad account was blocking its own customers
The auto-generated campaign had 602 negative keyword themes nobody had read. 119 were blocking real customer searches — the "near me" phrases, the service name itself, the neighbourhood name. Removed all 119.
Fixed
Budget was burning out in five days
Running at ₹400/day on broad, untargeted inventory. Rebuilt to ₹164/day on local search only, narrowed to three pincodes. Same reach where it counts, 59% less spend.
Fixed
18 of 20 reviews had no owner reply
Only the two angry ones had ever been answered. Wrote 13 individual replies referencing what each person actually said. Unreplied queue now empty — including a polite reply under both low ratings.
Fixed
Services were unsearchable
Entries read things like "Home Services" — phrases nobody types into Google. Rewrote all 8 using the words customers actually search, each with its own description.
Fixed
The Website button was dead
It pointed at a private editor URL — every click hit an error page, and Google had started guessing junk keywords because of it. Link removed, WhatsApp chat button kept.
Fixed
Posts kept getting rejected for a year
Cause: a phone number inside the post text, which breaks Google's policy. Same copy without the number, with a Call button instead, published instantly.
Fixed
Photos and the Products tab
Still empty. Listed here because a repair list that shows only wins is not a repair list.

What a check looks like

The 15 things we check on every listing

Each item is marked OK, Weak or Missing, and the listing gets a health score out of 100. Most listings we open score between 20 and 40.

  1. 01Profile claimed and verified
  2. 02Primary category — right one or nearest guess
  3. 03Secondary categories you are missing
  4. 04Services listed, and whether they are searchable
  5. 05Business description and the words in it
  6. 06Photo count and date of the last one
  7. 07Review count against your three nearest rivals
  8. 08How many reviews have an owner reply
  9. 09How recent your newest review is
  10. 10Date of your last post
  11. 11Website link — live, dead or missing
  12. 12WhatsApp / call buttons switched on
  13. 13Hours, including holidays
  14. 14Products tab and attributes left blank
  15. 15If you run ads — what they are actually buying
